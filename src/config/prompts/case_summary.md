Section: Summary Analysis of Cases

Present the total number of cases, new cases in the reporting period,
serious vs. non-serious breakdown, and patient/case characteristics
including age groups, sex distribution, geographic distribution.
For reaction frequency, label as: "Reaction frequency (case-level, each Preferred Term counted once per case)".
For outcome distribution, present the breakdown from the supplied evidence packet and note: "A single case may contribute multiple outcome entries."
CRITICAL RULE: Never describe reaction frequencies as "occurrence counts". Reaction frequencies represent case-level deduplicated counts (each Preferred Term counted once per case). Outcome frequencies may include multiple entries from the same case.
Target length: 150-250 words.
